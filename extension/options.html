MAILWEAVE · V1.0

Your conversation, clearly.

MailWeave processes email on this device. It stores only your automatic-opening preference, sidebar width and the email addresses you explicitly identify as yours.

One address per line. These senders use right-aligned pale green bubbles with left-aligned text. Gmail's active account is also recognized when available.

Close and reopen Chat after changing aliases. To use MailWeave, open a conversation at Gmail and select Chat.
